# ClipPilot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

ClipPilot is a desktop app for Windows and macOS that downloads, clips, subtitles and schedules videos, and posts them to TikTok for you. This policy explains what information ClipPilot handles and where it goes.

**The short version:** ClipPilot runs on your own computer. We don't run servers that receive your data, we don't collect analytics, and we don't sell or share anything about you. Your videos and TikTok login stay on your computer, except for the videos you choose to post, which go straight to TikTok.

## Information ClipPilot handles

All of this is stored only on your own computer, in ClipPilot's app data folder and the video folder you choose.

| Information | Why | Where it's kept |
|---|---|---|
| Your TikTok login tokens (from TikTok's official Login Kit) | To post videos to the TikTok account you connect | On your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage |
| Your TikTok display name, username, profile picture and posting options (from TikTok's API) | To show which account you're posting to and offer the visibility options TikTok allows | On your computer |
| Videos you import, download or create, and their subtitles | To clip, subtitle and post them | On your computer, in the folders you choose |
| Captions, hashtags, schedules and settings you enter | To post your videos at the times you choose | On your computer |
| Your TikTok developer client key and secret, if you enter them | To connect to TikTok's API | On your computer; the secret is encrypted |

ClipPilot never sees or stores your TikTok password. You sign in on TikTok's own website.

## Where information goes

ClipPilot only sends information to other services when it needs to do something you've asked for:

- **TikTok**: when a post is due, ClipPilot uploads that video, with its caption and the settings you chose, to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API. TikTok's [Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy) applies to anything you post there.
- **Video websites**: when you paste a link, ClipPilot downloads the video from that website, for example YouTube. That website sees a normal download request from your computer.
- **Hugging Face**: the first time you use subtitles, ClipPilot downloads a speech-recognition model from Hugging Face. Speech recognition then runs entirely on your computer. Your audio and videos are never uploaded for transcription.
- **GitHub**: ClipPilot downloads and updates its helper tools (yt-dlp and Deno) from their official GitHub releases.

We don't receive any of this information. ClipPilot contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting code.

## How long information is kept, and deleting it

Information stays on your computer until you remove it:

- **Disconnect a TikTok account**: open Accounts in ClipPilot and click Disconnect. ClipPilot deletes the saved login tokens and asks TikTok to revoke them. You can also remove ClipPilot's access in TikTok's own settings.
- **Delete videos**: remove them in ClipPilot's Library, or delete the files from your video folder.
- **Delete everything**: uninstall ClipPilot and delete its app data folder (Settings, then "Open app data folder" shows where it is).

## Your rights

Because ClipPilot keeps your information on your own device, you control it directly. If you're in the UK or EU you also have rights under data protection law, including the right to access, correct and delete personal data, and to complain to a regulator (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk). Contact us at the address above with any request.

## Children

ClipPilot is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age to hold a TikTok account where you live.
